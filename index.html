Master's thesis, 2021

Project organization change control in a small to medium-sized enterprise

My Master's thesis in project management at Turku University of Applied Sciences. I studied how internal and external changes affect customer delivery projects in an international small to medium-sized company.

The work was action research in three stages: a workshop that identified the changes that affect the whole project, a survey, and interviews that also collected ideas for improvement.

Result: a change management table for the project staff, a tool for spotting and understanding changes early. The main lessons were to record project reflections and use them before the next project starts, and to keep communication with stakeholders open and continuous.

Why it matters for my work: in delivery projects a change in scope or schedule reaches purchasing and logistics fast. Seeing it early saves time and money.
